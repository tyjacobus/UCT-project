Definitions (alphabetical)

Acceptance probability $p_C(x)$
$\sum_{y_m=1}|\amp{y}{C}{x0^{m-n}}|^2$. the definition of acceptance probability
Amplitude
A coordinate of a quantum state; $\braket z\psi$. the definition of a qubit, the definition of an $m$-qubit state
Bell state $\ket{\Phi^+}$
$\tfrac1{\sqrt2}(\ket{00}+\ket{11})$, an entangled state. Bell state, the Bell-state example
BPP
Bounded-error probabilistic polynomial time. the definition of $\mathsf{BPP}$
BQP
Bounded-error quantum polynomial time: uniform circuits over $\mathcal G$ with $p\ge\frac23$ on yes-instances and $\le\frac13$ on no-instances. the definition of $\mathsf{BQP}$
Classical (permutation) gate
A gate that maps each basis state to a basis state; $X$, CNOT, TOF. the definition of classical gates
CNOT, Toffoli
$\ket{a,b}\mapsto\ket{a,b\oplus a}$; $\ket{a,b,c}\mapsto\ket{a,b,c\oplus ab}$. the definition of CNOT and Toffoli
Quantum circuit
A sequence of gates; computes $C = U_T\cdots U_1$. the definition of a quantum circuit
Entangled / product state
Product: $\ket a\otimes\ket b$; entangled: not a product. the definition of entanglement
Follow
The classical path-following procedure. the definition of $\mathrm{Follow}$
Gate set $\mathcal G$
$\{H,X,\CNOT,\TOF\}$. the definition of our gate set $\mathcal G$; universality: the universality theorem for Hadamard + Toffoli
Gate applied to chosen qubits
$G_{[j_1..j_k]}$ acts on the chosen bits and carries the rest along. the definition of applying a gate to chosen qubits
Hadamard gate $H$; Hadamard formula
$H\ket a = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}\sum_c(-1)^{ac}\ket c$. the definition of the single-qubit gates, the Hadamard Formula
Uniform superposition
$H^{\otimes n}\ket{0^n} = 2^{-n/2}\sum_y\ket y$. the Uniform Superposition Lemma
Inner product, norm, conjugate transpose
the definition of inner product and norm
Interference
Cancellation (destructive) or reinforcement (constructive) of path weights that end at the same basis state. the Sum-over-Paths Lemma, the interference principle
Measurement (Born rule)
Outcome $z$ with probability $|\braket z\psi|^2$; one qubit: $\sum_{z_j=1}|\braket z\psi|^2$. the Born rule, the measurement rule
Path-sum lemma
$C\ket z = 2^{-h/2}\sum_b(-1)^{\varphi(z,b)}\ket{w(z,b)}$. the Path-Sum Lemma
PP
Probabilistic polynomial time: accept with probability $>\frac12$ exactly on yes-instances. Mentioned only in the further results
Qubit; $m$-qubit state
A unit vector in $\C^2$; in $\C^{2^m}$. the definition of a qubit, the definition of an $m$-qubit state
Reversible simulation; uncomputation
the Reversible Simulation Lemma, Bennett's Uncomputation Lemma
Stochastic matrix
the definition of stochastic matrices
Sum over paths
the Sum-over-Paths Lemma
Tensor product
the definition of the tensor product, the properties of the tensor product
Uniform circuit family
the definition of uniform circuit families
Unitary matrix
$U^\dagger U = I$; equivalently norm-preserving. the definition of a unitary matrix, the fact that unitary means norm-preserving
$X$ and $Z$ gates
the definition of the single-qubit gates
